🛤️ Recommended Learning Flow

Learn step-by-step instead of trying to learn everything at once.

1

Understand

Start with the basic concepts and understand why the skill matters.

2

Learn

Follow beginner-friendly lessons and learning resources.

3

Practise

Solve exercises and practise the concepts you learned.

4

Build

Create a small project to apply your knowledge.

💡 ProSync Learning Philosophy

You don't need to master every skill immediately. Start with one skill, learn the basics, practise it and apply it through a small project. Your learning journey can grow gradually as your interests become clearer.
